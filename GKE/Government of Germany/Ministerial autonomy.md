<h2>Meaning</h2>

> Ministerial autonomy means that each minister in a government has the independent authority to *run their own ministry without interference from other ministers* as long as they follow the overall rules set by the government.

Ministers can
- make decisions independently
- issue regulations  
- manage personnel  
- set priorities 
- run day‑to‑day operations

without needing approval by any other ministers

---

<h2>Effects</h2>

*Ministerial autonomy* enures:
- *clear responsibilities* because every minister is only responsible for his/her own ministry
- *efficiency* due to a better focus on specific subjects
- *specialization* because experts lead specific fields
