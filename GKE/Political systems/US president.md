<h2>Role</h2>

The US president acts as the *head of state* **and** as the *head of government* giving him a powerful role within the [[Political systems - US|political system of the US]].

---

<h2>Relationship to legislative & executive</h2>

> - is independant
> - requires legislative backup
> - is chief of executive
> - can be impeached

The president requires *legislative backup for passing laws* but is still *indipendant* when it comes to creating new laws. There is **no** *vote-of-no-confidence* but instead there is a system called *impeachment* present in the US political system.

In the US system the president is the *chief of the executive* and the *head of state*, giving him *prestige* and making him *"appear everywhere"*.

--- 

<h2>Relationship to the cabinet & other parties</h2>

> - does not lead a party
> - the Cabinet is much less significant

In comparison to [[GB prime minister|Great Britains prime minister]] the US president *does not lead a party* although he is part of a party in most cases similar to the [[German chancellor]].  

In the US the Cabinet is much *less significant in legislation* as the US *president is able to pass laws on his own* as long as he has legislative backup.

---

<h2>Ability to achieve desired policies</h2>

> - might be difficult
> - needs approval of congress & $2/3$ of Senate

In the US system passing laws might be *difficult* for the president as he needs approval of *2/3 of the senate* and *approval by congress* which can be quite hard as he *does not always have a majority in the congress*.

---

<h2>Length of term</h2>

> 4 years

The US president has a *fixed term* of *4 years* and *can be reelected once* making his maximum time in office $2 \cdot 4 \text{ years}$.
